Since 2016

A workshop that grew into a home for slow living

Crib Furniture started as a two-person workshop in Nairobi in 2016, building one custom piece at a time for neighbours and friends. Today we work with over 40 local artisans across Kenya to hand-build furniture meant to be lived on, not just looked at.

Every piece is made from responsibly sourced hardwood, finished by hand, and inspected before it leaves the workshop. We believe furniture should feel like it already belongs in your home — warm, unpretentious, and built to outlast trends.
